What to notice

  • The chance of matching exactly k numbers is C(6, k) C(43, 6 − k) / C(49, 6): choose which of your numbers were drawn, then choose the rest from the numbers you did not pick.
  • About 44% of tickets match nothing at all, and matching three or more happens less than 2% of the time.
  • At one ticket a week, a jackpot is expected about once every 268,000 years. 100,000 simulated weeks is under 2,000 years of play — almost certainly not enough.
