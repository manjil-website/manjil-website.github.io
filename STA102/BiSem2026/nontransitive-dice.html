Every pair, head to head

The chance that the row die beats the column die. There are no ties: no two dice share a face value.

What to notice

  • A beats B, B beats C, C beats D, and D beats A — each with probability 2/3. “Beats” is not transitive.
  • Check D against A by splitting into cases: D shows 5 (probability 1/2) and always wins; D shows 1 (probability 1/2) and wins only if A shows 0 (probability 1/3). Total 1/2 + 1/6 = 2/3. That case split is the law of total probability.
  • The lesson for games: always let your opponent choose first.
